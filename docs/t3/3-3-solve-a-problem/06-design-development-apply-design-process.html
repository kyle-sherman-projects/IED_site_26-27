Design development: apply design process

Class 6 of 8

Systems thinking; predict local/global impacts

02 Today's question

?What questions should you ask yourself before beginning a project?

In other words, asking the right questions at the start saves you from building the wrong thing.

03 Words for today
systems thinkingLooking at how parts interact as a whole, instead of at one piece in isolation.
black box modelA way of describing a system by its inputs and outputs without detailing what's inside.
unintended consequenceAn effect of a design nobody planned for, often showing up far from the original decision.
CADComputer-Aided Design — designing a part as a model on a computer instead of on paper.
04 Learning target

By the end of today you will be able to:

Apply the black box model to your selected design concept to map all inputs, processes, and outputs (including waste and unintended effects), predict at least one unintended consequence, and begin translating the selected concept into a CAD model or detailed dimensioned sketch.

05 What you'll produce

By end of class you'll submit: a completed systems map (black box model) of your selected concept with all input/output streams labeled (including waste, energy, and user effects) and at least one unintended consequence identified and analyzed. Additionally, you'll have a begun CAD model or detailed dimensioned concept sketch with at least the major geometry started.
