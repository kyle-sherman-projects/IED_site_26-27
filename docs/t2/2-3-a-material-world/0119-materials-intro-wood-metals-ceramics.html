PLTW 2.3.1  ·  Lesson 2.3 — A Material World

Materials intro: wood, metals, ceramics, plastics, composites

Tue, Jan 19 Day A 80 min

Properties that drive selection

02 Today's question

?How do you determine the properties of a material?

03 Learning target

By the end of today you will be able to:

Identify the five material families and your defining engineering properties (tensile strength, hardness, ductility, thermal/electrical conductivity, density, cost, machinability), then sort candidate materials by property to support a criteria-based selection decision.

04 What you'll produce

By the end of class, you'll have a completed Material Property Reference Chart — a single-page table listing ≥10 materials (at least 2 from each family), with approximate values for tensile strength, hardness (qualitative scale), ductility (qualitative), density, electrical conductivity, and relative cost. The chart is legible, uses consistent units, and will be kept as a reference tool for the remainder of the unit.
